REIMBURSEMENT TEST DATA - Reimbursement Sample 2
==========================================================
A train trip with a hotel stay and a mobile bill: some bills fall outside the policy. All bills are fictional.

HOW TO TEST (Reimbursement & Benefits demo, Manual · Live)
  1. Type your employee ID or work e-mail when the chat asks (any onboarded employee works; the claim is saved for that person).
  2. Upload the 3 PDFs in this folder as the bills, one at a time (use "Add another bill" after each).
  3. Check that each bill is read as shown below, then submit the claim.
  4. On the Approvals page (Reimbursement tab) approve or reject each bill as the manager.
  5. The approved bills are then recorded in OrangeHRM (Claim).

WHAT EACH BILL SHOULD BE READ AS
  file                         category policy          date        amount
  1_Train_Ticket.pdf           Travel   within policy   22/10/2026  INR     668.60   Indian Railways e-Ticket (IRCTC)
  2_Hotel_Bill.pdf             Stay     outside policy  24/10/2026  INR   7,168.00   Hotel Sunrise Residency, Mumbai

  Claim total: INR 8,585.90

NOTES
  - "Within policy" means the category the company reimburses (Travel and Food). The manager can still approve any bill.
  - The category is decided by the reader (OpenAI) from the bill itself; if it reads a bill differently from the table, tell me which one.
  - Amounts are the final amount on each bill, with tax. Dates are printed as DD/MM/YYYY.
  - Suggested checks: approve every bill once; reject one bill and approve the others; try uploading a file that is not a bill (for example 0_Claim_Details.txt) to see it refused.
